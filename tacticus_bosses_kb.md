## Main Boss: Avatar of Khaine
- **Faction:** Aeldari
- **Associated Side Bosses (Primes):** Aethana Barantharal, Eldryon Ynaduin
- **Movement:** 3
- **Normal Melee Attack:** 2x Melta Damage (75% pierce ratio)
- **Normal Ranged Attack:** Range 2, 1x Melta Damage (75% pierce ratio)
- **Traits:**
  - **Boss**
  - **Immune**: This unit is immune to special hexes and hex effects, and cannot have its Armour, hits, Movement or Range reduced. Additionally, this unit cannot be Stunned, Suppressed or Taunted, and does not receive knockback damage.
  - **Big Target**: Due to its size, this unit benefits less from cover but also provides some obstruction to enemy sighting. Does not get any bonuses from Tall Grass and Trenches. Adjacent friendly units receive -1 hit from ranged attacks, to a minimum of 1. Cannot be displaced.
  - **Daemon**: This unit has an additional 25% chance to Block up to 50% of its own current Damage stat.

  Faces in one of 6 directions and sets a cone of hexes in range 2 in front of it on Fire, dealing 3x [10 .. 982]-[12 .. 1179] Flame Damage to all enemies on affected hexes. At the beginning of its next turn, deals 2x [36 .. 3536]-[45 .. 4420] Melta Damage to all enemies on affected hexes.
- **The Wailing Doom Strikes** *(Ranged)*:
  Faces in one of 6 directions and sets all hexes in a straight line 2 hexes wide and originating from the Avatar of Khaine on Fire, dealing 3x [10 .. 982]-[12 .. 1179] Flame Damage to all enemies on affected hexes. ### Active Abilities
- **Needs More Dakka!** *(Ranged)*:
  Ghazghkull faces in one of 6 directions and loads up Mork's Roar as he prepares to open fire on an area in range 3 in front of him. At the beginning of his next turn, all units in the marked area receive 8x [20 .. 1964]-[30 .. 2946] Projectile Damage while he unleashes an intense hail of bullets. Targets adjacent to Ghazghkull receive 4x additional hits. Friendly Orks receive 50% fewer hits.
- **Da Great Waaagh!**:
  Ghazghkull summons 1 Stormboy for each enemy on the map plus 5 additional Ork Boyz, who appear on free hexes near the top of the map. Then each Ork takes a normal turn, gaining +1 Movement and +1 hit on their melee attacks this turn.
- **'Ere We Go!** *(Melee)*:
  Ghazghkull faces in one of 6 directions and prepares to charge out to range 5 in front of him. At the start of his next turn he charges along that path, leaving devastation in his wake. Enemy Summons in the path are automatically defeated. Each enemy Character on the path takes 1x [50 .. 4911]-[60 .. 5893] Direct Damage and is Stunned for 1 round. Orks in the path also get Stunned for 1 round but take no Damage. All units defeated by this ability are always overkilled.

### Passive Abilities
- **Proper Killy**:
  Always eager to engage the toughest foe, Ghazghkull's melee attack prioritises the unit with the highest current health total. He slashes with Gork's Klaw, attacking all enemies in the 3 hexes in front of him. Each target takes 1 hit that deals 60% of their Max Health as Power Damage. When damage is dealt this way, each target hit is also Stunned for 1 round. Afterwards each target is hit with Ghazghkull's normal melee attack.
- **Prophet of Gork and Mork**:
  After being attacked 4 times in a turn, Ghazghkull takes -90% Damage until the end of that turn.
- **Da Boss Is Watchin'**:
  Adjacent Orks ignore Battle Fatigue and deal +20% Damage.

### Prime Debuffs (Applied to Boss when Side Bosses are defeated)
- Da Great Waaagh! summons 1 fewer Ork Boyz
- Da Great Waaagh! summons 2 fewer Ork Boyz
- Needs More Dakka! scores 1 fewer hits
- Needs More Dakka! scores 2 fewer hits
- Proper Killy score 1 fewer hits
- +1  attacks needed to trigger Prophet of Gork and Mork
- +2  attacks needed to trigger Prophet of Gork and Mork
